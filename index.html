How this tracker works

  1. Phases — the launch is broken into ordered phases. Click a phase in the rail to jump to it.
  2. Steps — click any step to open it. Inside you get official links, agency contacts, a sub-checklist, a status, an owner, a due date, a cost field, a running notes log, and a next-steps list.
  3. Your data — everything saves automatically in this browser (localStorage). It is not sent anywhere and no one else can see it.
  4. Back it up — click Export to download a JSON file. Use Import to restore it, or to move your progress to another computer or browser. Do this regularly; clearing your browser data wipes the tracker.
  5. Adding your own — every phase has an “Add step” button, and every step has “Add contact”. Custom items are yours and survive updates to the built-in content.

A caution on the pre-loaded details

Phone numbers, fees, and rules were gathered from Salt Lake County Health Department, Salt Lake City, and Utah state sources. Fee amounts marked est. come from third-party guides and are not official quotes. Confirm everything with the agency before you spend money or make plans. Requirements also differ by city — Salt Lake City, West Valley, Sandy, Murray and unincorporated county are not the same.
